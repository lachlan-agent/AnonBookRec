Anonymous Book Recomendations

About

I absolutely despise services that require excessive amounts of personal information for the simplest of tasks. That's why I made this I just wanted a simple, no-nonsense, easy way to recieve book recommendations. Please keep in mind that this is a very early trial of the page. If you experience any problems or bugs please contact me (see details below).

How it works

Create a page. Share the link. People then add books using the link. Pretty simple.

Privacy

No email, no personal information, no malicious tracking. Your page is keyed by a secret only you hold. Recommendations marked as private are encrypted so that even the server's database reveals nothing of interest. Lose the key and they're gone; that's the point. Optionally, you may choose a username and password. Note, that the recovery process is the same: retain your key or it's gone.
